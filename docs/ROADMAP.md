# Product Roadmap — GradeStar

This document outlines the product vision, current release capabilities, and planned user-facing enhancements for **GradeStar**.

---

## 1. Product Vision & Mission

Navigating university and college courses often requires managing complex grading schemes, uneven weights, and shifting assignment deadlines across multiple course syllabi. Standard university portals and learning management systems (LMS) often present cluttered, confusing grade books that leave students wondering about their actual standing or what marks they need on upcoming finals to reach their goals.

**GradeStar** provides a fast, distraction-free, and visually engaging academic dashboard where students can:
1. **See Their Standing Instantly**: View overall course marks and category averages at a single glance through clean visual indicators.
2. **Organize Deliverables by Weight**: Group homework, labs, quizzes, and exams into custom grading categories that match course outlines.
3. **Never Miss a Deadline**: Track course due dates on an integrated, easy-to-read monthly calendar.
4. **Plan for Success**: Experiment with predictive "what-if" scenarios to discover the exact scores needed on remaining exams to hit target letter grades.

---

## 2. Current Status: What's Live Today

Students can currently use GradeStar to establish their academic hub and organize course basics:

- **Secure Personal Account**: Create an account and sign in securely with real-time feedback ensuring strong password protection.
- **Courses Dashboard**: View all enrolled courses in an organized visual grid showcasing course titles, catalog codes (e.g., CS 101), and custom color accents.
- **Quick Course Setup**: Add new courses with a streamlined popup dialog specifying title, course code, and start date.
- **Course Detail View**: Open any course to access an all-in-one view featuring:
  - **Visual Grade Wheel**: A circular percentage indicator that highlights your overall grade standing.
  - **Deliverables Overview**: A ranked list of assignments and exams organized by grade weight.
  - **Monthly Course Calendar**: A dynamic monthly calendar highlighting today's date and marking days with upcoming assignment deadlines.
- **Distraction-Free Experience**: A modern, responsive sidebar navigation that works smoothly across screen sizes.

---

## 3. Planning Horizon

The following is the list of planned user-facing features and enhancements for GradeStar:

- **Interactive Assignment & Deliverable Tracking**:
  - Add assignments, quizzes, labs, and exams directly within a course with custom titles, due dates, earned scores, and total points possible (e.g., 42/50).
  - Add standalone major deliverables (like a midterm or final exam) without needing to place them into a category group.
  - One-click completion and grading toggles to mark assignments as submitted or graded.
  - Update assignment details or delete dropped deliverables with instant updates to course averages.

- **Custom Syllabus Categories & Weighting**:
  - Group deliverables into weighted categories matching course syllabi (e.g., "Homework 20%", "Labs 25%", "Final Exam 40%").
  - Choose between splitting category weight evenly across assignments automatically or assigning custom weight percentages to individual items.

- **Real-Time Grade Calculations**:
  - Automatic recalculation of overall course grades whenever a score is entered or updated.
  - Dual grade views: toggle between **Current Standing** (average on graded work completed so far) and **Total Course Progress** (points earned toward the full 100% course total).
  - Dynamic visual grade wheel that reflects your calculated percentage in real time.

- **Course Settings & Customization**:
  - Edit course names, course codes, custom color themes, and semester date ranges at any time.
  - Archive completed courses to keep your dashboard clean, or permanently delete courses you are no longer taking.

- **Interactive Monthly Calendar**:
  - Navigate forward and backward across months to review past deadlines and plan for upcoming test periods.
  - Click on any calendar day marked with a deadline to open a details popover showing all due tasks, their grade weight, and direct shortcuts to mark them completed.

- **Semester & Term Organization**:
  - Group courses on the dashboard under distinct semester headers (e.g., "Fall 2026", "Winter 2027").
  - Filter or switch views to focus only on your active term or browse past semesters.

- **"What-If" Grade Simulator & Goal Forecaster**:
  - Target grade calculator: enter a desired final grade (e.g., 85% or an "A") to see the exact scores needed on remaining assignments and exams.
  - Hypothetical score testing: experiment with prospective exam marks in a sandbox mode without altering saved course records.

- **Cumulative GPA & Academic Overview**:
  - Main dashboard widget displaying overall cumulative GPA or combined academic average across all courses.
  - Support for multiple university grading scales (4.0 scale, 4.33 scale, letter grades A+ through F, percentage scales).

- **Course Sharing & Syllabus Templates**:
  - Share pre-configured course grading schemes with classmates taking the same class so they can set up their courses in seconds.
  - Download a personal backup copy of your courses, deliverables, and grades for safekeeping.

- **Learning Management System (LMS) Sync**:
  - Connect with platforms like Canvas, Brightspace (D2L), and Blackboard to automatically pull in enrolled courses, grading breakdowns, and assignment due dates.
  - Upload a syllabus document or PDF to automatically detect and extract grading categories and weights.

- **Deadline Reminders & Study Alerts**:
  - Timely alerts 24 and 48 hours before an assignment or exam is due so deadlines are never missed.
  - Weekly study digest highlighting all upcoming deliverables across enrolled courses for the week ahead.

- **Visual Personalization & Theming**:
  - Switch between a sleek dark theme for late-night study sessions and a clean light theme for daytime reading.
  - Expanded selection of vibrant course accent colors and visual tags.

- **Anywhere Access & Offline Mode**:
  - View courses, check schedules, and review deliverables even without an internet connection, with automatic syncing once reconnected.
  - Install GradeStar directly to phone, tablet, or desktop home screens for quick app-like access.
